About All Job Alert SL

All Job Alert SL connects job seekers across Sierra Leone with real, verified opportunities, jobs, scholarships, training, workshops, masterclasses, and seminars, all in one place.

Why we started

Too many people miss out on opportunities simply because they never hear about them, or because scam postings make it hard to trust what's real. We built this platform to close that gap, one place where opportunities from every district are collected, checked, and shared for free.

How it works

Anyone can post an opportunity. Before it goes live, our team reviews it to help filter out scams and fake listings. Once approved, it's visible to everyone searching or browsing by district and category.

Our promise

Never pay money to apply for a job through this platform. If a listing asks for payment to "process" your application, treat it as a red flag and report it to us using the feedback form.

Stay connected

Have a suggestion, a question, or something you'd like to see improved? Use the feedback form on our homepage, or join our WhatsApp channel for updates on the latest opportunities.
